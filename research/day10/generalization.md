# Day 10 — Generalization Matrix (structural-pressure case generation)

Purpose: stop optimizing against a random zoo; cases were generated by STRUCTURAL PRESSURE cells
so the regression suite spanned the space the hidden set could plausibly sample.

## Dimensions (all observable at parse time)

- contention: rhoE, rhoC (decode demand / capacity over T)
- arrival density: mean arrivals/tick, burstiness (peak window vs mean), arrival spread vs T
- deadline pressure: mean slack_ratio, tight-cluster fraction (D - A near chain time)
- decode depth: Lout distribution (mean, Lout>=7 fraction), hop length vs T
- communication pressure: COMM/PEDGE ratio, per-site hop split
- scale: n, T, E, C, B (capacity vs n)
- horizon pressure: chain-window vs remaining-T at admit time (the Day-9 quantity)

## Cell plan (7 dims, 2-3 levels each — generate only the informative corners)

| #   | cell                      | levels                                                   | why                                                                          |
| --- | ------------------------- | -------------------------------------------------------- | ---------------------------------------------------------------------------- |
| 1   | n x rhoE                  | high-N+low-rhoE, high-N+high-rhoE, low-N+extreme-T       | bigpool vs burst separation (Day-9: starve vs displacement)                  |
| 2   | arrival x deadline        | burst+loose, burst+tight-mix, spread+loose, spread+tight | flips need burst+loose to exist; tight-mix is the displacement victim family |
| 3   | decode depth x contention | Lout>=7-heavy + E=3, Lout-light + E=3, heavy + E=6       | capacity ratio E/n controls starve-vs-drain                                  |
| 4   | T x burstiness            | T=150-300 bursts, T=1000-4000 spread, T near max         | Day-9: threshold needs scale key                                             |
| 5   | COMM x PCLOUD             | COMM/PEDGE=1x, 3x, 10x                                   | comm-bound classes (comm_*: all-gain, keep them in the gate)                 |
| 6   | imbalance                 | 3E<C, 3C<E, balanced                                     | IMBAL gate class tracking                                                    |
| 7   | horizon                   | slack_ratio 0.1-0.25 vs 0.25-0.5 vs >0.5                 | the Day-9 bucket that failed — keep sampling it, never gate on it            |

## Protocol

- Each cell: >= 30 cases (seeded), reused via --pool; cells registered in the DEV pool.
- Every mechanism-specific cell must use `matrix_gen.py --require MECHANISM:CASES` and retain
  its manifest. The generator records clamp/late/deep/burst/contention counts and exits
  non-zero when the requested mechanism did not reproduce. Use
  `--require burst_displacement:CASES` for the structural preconditions of the Day-9
  burst mechanism (clamp + late/deep + burst), and `--require starvation_tail:CASES`
  for the large-scale long-horizon, spread, deep-decode preconditions. These are
  generator-level preconditions, not proof that a particular solver will flip, displace,
  or starve: validate the observed outcome separately. A score-neutral cell without a
  satisfied mechanism assertion is a blind spot, not evidence.

- Freeze gate stays cases/*.txt top-level (20) — the matrix EXTENDS class-tracking pools,
  it does not replace the gate.

- Per-freeze expectation: no class regression beyond tolerance in ANY cell (cells are the
  "class" unit for the selection discipline).

- New candidate families (estfix-class changes) must pass the matrix BEFORE any freeze
  evaluation; a candidate that wins +500 on one cell while losing a cell is not freezeable.

## Relationship to Day-9 findings

- The burst-loss family = cell (2)x(1): burst arrivals + loose D + E=3 + short T.
  The bigpool-loss family = cell (4)x(3): spread arrivals + long T + deep decode + high n.
  These are DIFFERENT phenomena (displacement vs starvation); the matrix keeps them
  separately observable instead of averaged into "bigpool"/"burst".

## Regime evidence (EXP-36, 2026-08-06)

Matrix cells REPRODUCE the Day-9 phenomena (frozen vs estfix, 40 cases/cell):

| cell              | params                                                          | flips | delta            | regime                                            |
| ----------------- | --------------------------------------------------------------- | ----- | ---------------- | ------------------------------------------------- |
| burst_clusters    | n 16-40, T 150-200, 3-6 late clusters, rho 1.0-2.2 clamped, E=3 | 200   | **-24.005 FAIL** | displacement (matches dev burst -11/31)           |
| burst_loose_EARLY | same, arrivals in [1, 0.4T]                                     | -     | +40.993          | all-gain — arrival position is a regime dimension |
| big_spread        | n=96, T=2400, Lout 7-16, spread                                 | 76    | +75.994          | bigpool-like gain (starvation tail needs n>100)   |
| big_clusters      | n=96, T=2400, clusters                                          | 12    | +27.996          | bigpool-like gain                                 |

Key mechanism behind all of it: flips are produced by the T-CLAMP (D=min(T, A+rho*dur),
late arrival + deep Lout => D=T < old_est). Generators that never clamp produce zero flips
and byte-identical scores — a silent regime miss.

Open cells: big_spread at n=150-300 (starvation tail), E=6 vs E=3 at big-N (scale key),
tight-mix displacement victims (rho near 1.0 clustered with deep).
